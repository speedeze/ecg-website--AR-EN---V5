About Us

About Egyptian Consulting Group

Egyptian Consulting Group (ECG) is a specialized professional expert house offering integrated solutions for companies and investors, including external audit, tax consulting, financial statement preparation, feasibility studies, financial restructuring, company valuation and setup, and ERP system implementation such as Odoo.

🎯 Our Vision

To be the leading expert house in providing precise and professional financial and tax consulting that supports the growth and stability of the business sector in Egypt.

💡 Our Mission

We believe that financial success starts with governance and transparency; thus, we are committed to delivering services built on the highest quality standards to build long-term partnerships with our clients.

Executive & Professional Leadership

Mr. Khaled Gaber Mahmoud

Certified Accountant & Auditor | Founder & Managing Director (ECG)

He has over 25 years of professional experience in accounting, auditing, financial, and tax consulting within the Arab Republic of Egypt. He possesses extensive practical experience in applying Egyptian Accounting Standards and International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS), along with deep familiarity with Egyptian tax legislation, enabling him to provide professional solutions that enhance compliance, improve financial performance, and empower decision-making.
